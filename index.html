05 — Selected work · 01 / 03
Healthcare AI Triage

UrgentCareX

Urgent care, triaged in seconds.

An AI-powered healthcare triage system built around one goal: get people to the right urgent care, fast. Patients describe what's happening in plain language; UrgentCareX assesses urgency, routes them to the appropriate level of care, and takes the guesswork out of “is this an emergency?” — so the right help reaches the right person without delay.

05 — Selected work · 02 / 03
EdTech Behavioral Health PBIS

BehaveBridge

AI in the classroom corner — PBIS, by the book.

BehaveBridge connects teachers and behavioral experts with parents in one shared space — but its real engine is AI that supports teachers in the moment. When a situation arises in the classroom, BehaveBridge helps educators follow the correct PBIS (Positive Behavioral Interventions & Supports) structure — guiding the right response, documentation, and follow-through. Less guesswork under pressure; consistent, evidence-based intervention every time.
